{%- comment -%} Plain-text summary of a post, for the blog list and the meta description. Uses the post's `summary` front matter if it has one; otherwise the text of its first paragraph, so headings, images and lists at the top of a post never end up in the summary. Parameters: post (a post or page), words (truncate to this many words). {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.post.summary -%} {{ include.post.summary | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | truncatewords: include.words }} {%- else -%} {%- assign paragraphs = include.post.content | split: '

' -%} {{ paragraphs[1] | split: '

' | first | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | truncatewords: include.words }} {%- endif -%}
